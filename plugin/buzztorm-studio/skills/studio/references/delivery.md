# Delivery package

Files (SendUserFile) + `delivery/post.md` (also shown in chat in a code block). Respect the Options: what is OFF is left out.

## File names
`<brand>_<format>_<title>_v<N>_<size>.<ext>` — lowercase, hyphens inside parts, underscores between, size `9x16` · `4x5` · `1x1` · `16x9` · `letter`; slides/frames end `_01`, `_02`…
e.g. `buzztorm_carousel_content-plan_v2_4x5_03.png` · `buzztorm_checklist_community-launch-checklist_v1_letter.pdf`

## post.md sections (only those the recipe's `copy` lists)
`# <Brand> · <Format> · <title> · v<N>` then `## Instagram`, `## TikTok`, `## YouTube Shorts`, `## YouTube`, `## LinkedIn`, `## Ad 1…`, `## Story` (sticker plan), `## Discord`, `## Pinned comment`, `## Alt text`, `## Cover`, `## Posting note`.
The Quality Gate counts hashtags per section and checks the Instagram first line.

## Post copy formula (Social)
1. Line 1 continues the hook (≤ 125 characters, shows before "more").
2. Lines 2–3: payoff or value.
3. A question that makes them think of someone to send it to (never "tag a friend").
4. The CTA from `brain/cta.json` (Teach, Sell, Community).
TikTok: 1–2 lines + question. Shorts: title-like first line + 1 line.

## Hashtags — 1·1·3
1 brand + 1 series (if any) + up to 3 niche/topic from the Brand Profile. Max 5 Instagram (platform limit), 3–5 TikTok, 3 YouTube and LinkedIn, 3 for memes. None on Story, Discord, sales page video or ads. Never generic (#viral, #fyp).

## Other formats
YouTube: 3 titles (≤ 60 characters), description with chapters from scene times (00:00 first, ≥ 3, each ≥ 10 s), thumbnail. LinkedIn: hook line, 2–3 short paragraphs, a question, 3 hashtags. Sales page video: headline (≤ 10 words), subheadline, poster frame. Story: per frame text + sticker (type, exact text, position). Documents: a 4:5 promo post for the lead magnet + post copy with the comment keyword.

## Posting note
Always one line: where/how to post (e.g. "Post as trial reels; log results in 72 h", "Turn on the ACADEMY comment automation first").

## Instagram caption structure (default for reels and carousels)

Write every Instagram caption in this order. Short lines, one idea per line.

1. **Hook question** (first line, ≤125 characters): the reader's pain as a question, plus a pointer to the video ("Watch the egg again.").
2. **Concrete scene or proof**: what happened in the video, or a sourced fact from facts.json.
3. **The turn**: two contrasting lines ("People don't stay for X. / They stay for Y.").
4. **Why the usual way fails**: one or two lines.
5. **Save line**: "Save this question 👇" with ❌ the usual question and ✅ the better question.
6. **The answer**: the idea, then what the brand does (from offers.json, no invented promises).
7. **CTA**: the DM keyword or link from cta.json, with the risk reversal if the offer has one.
8. **Send trigger**: "Now tell me: who do you know who…? 😅"
9. **Source line** when a fact was used: "(Study: …, year.)"
10. **Hashtags**: max 5, brand tag first.
