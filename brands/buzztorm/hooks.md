## Ideas
- History hook: "In 1518, in Strasbourg, hundreds of people started dancing in the street… and couldn't stop for weeks." → Tony's posting schedule.
- History hook: "In 1935, a chain letter almost sank the Denver post office." → word of mouth vs paid ads.
- Paradox: "The busiest gym in town never runs ads."
- Wrong on purpose: "Posting every day is killing your business."
- Impossible number: "$200 per client. Every year it goes up."
- Question with a stake: "What happens to your clients if you disappear for a week?"
- Freeze frame: "Yep. That's me." on Tony mid-disaster.
