# Buzztorm — series guide

## The series
"Tony did it again": every episode, Tony (celebrity fitness coach) runs into a growth problem that Buzztorm's idea solves. Dex narrates with dry humour.

## Characters
| Key | Name | Role | Never changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| D | Dex | Narrator | Navy casual jacket, dark tee, beige chinos, Pomp hair + chin beard |
| T | Tony | Hero | Blond hair, coral bandana, gold chain, black "TF" tank, coral shorts |
| M | Tony's Mom | Supporting | Raspberry-pink sweater, glasses, short grey hair |

## Running gags
- The ring light (Tony can never turn it off).
- Tony's bandana.
- The treadmill: posting every day = running and going nowhere.
- Tony's Mom shares everything (except Tony).

## Audience archetypes (who the viewer sends it to)
- The coach who posts at 5 a.m.
- The friend who pays for every client with ads.
- The friend who buys every course.
- The mom who shares everything on the family group.

## Catchphrases
- "Can I finally take off the ring light?"
- "Tony did it again."
