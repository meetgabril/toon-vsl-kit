# Buzztorm — brand rules

Rules that apply only to Buzztorm content, on top of the Studio Standards. They came from the approved VSL "Coach Tony's Treadmill" (v17).

## Characters
- Professional, masculine poses for male leads. No cartoonish flailing, nothing that "looks weird".
- Extras are clearly male or female, in bright fitness clothes and colourful tops, as on `ref/sheet_extras.jpg`. (Brand choice: extras use light skin tones. This is a Buzztorm rule, not a studio default.)
- Outfits never change between scenes unless the story says so. Tony is shirtless only at the beach.
- Tony and Dex are the same height in a two-shot.
- Logos and avatars inside UI cards show the real character (Tony's face on the ad-account card), never a generic person.

## Scenes
- Recording scenes show a real setup: camera on a tripod, softbox or ring light, phone rig. "REC" appears only on those.
- Show no website or phone UI except the brand's own. Say "community", never a platform's brand name.
- Data must make story sense: if a line asks "did my leads disappear?", the screen answers it ("checking…" → +47 new leads).

## Colour meaning
- Orange (accent) = Buzztorm and calls to action. Green = growth. Red = loss or a flagged account.

## Buzztorm backgrounds (built into the engine)
`stage` (Buzztorm studio), `casino` (the paid-ads world), `doors` (ADS / CONTENT), `community`, plus Tony's `gym`, `content`, `night` and `beach`.
