# Buzztorm — voice

- Confident, warm, dry humour. Sitcom energy, never preachy, never salesy.
- Plain spoken English, short sentences (15 words max), numbers as words in scripts.
- Say "community", never a platform's brand name. Say "members", not "users".
- The idea is the hero; Buzztorm appears at the turning point or the CTA, never as an ad break.
- Avoid: "hustle", "passive income", "guaranteed", "game-changer", "unlock", "secret".
- Good: "Followers become members. Members become buyers. Buyers bring new members."
- Bad: "Unlock passive income with our game-changing community system!"
