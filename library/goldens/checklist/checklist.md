## Before launch
- [ ] **One promise.** Finish this sentence: "Members get ___ in ___ weeks." No promise, no community.
- [ ] **One home.** Pick one place for members. Not your DMs, not three apps.
- [ ] **Three rooms only.** Start with #welcome, #wins and #questions. Add more when they are full.
- [ ] **Your first 15.** List 15 people by name who already like your work. Invite them personally.
- [ ] **A welcome path.** Write the first message every new member sees, with one action: introduce yourself.

## Launch week
- [ ] **Day-one win.** Give members something they can finish in 10 minutes and post in #wins.
- [ ] **Visible activity.** Reply to every introduction within 24 hours. People join where others are already talking.
- [ ] **A weekly ritual.** Same day, same time: a live, a challenge or a Q&A.

## After launch
- [ ] **Wins on repeat.** Share one member win every week, with permission.
- [ ] **Members invite members.** Give them a reason and a link to bring a friend.
- [ ] **Offer inside, not instead.** Your paid offer lives inside the community as the next step.
- [ ] **One number.** Track weekly active members. Not followers.
